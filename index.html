About

I'm completing an MSc in Software Design with Cybersecurity at the Technological University of the Shannon (TUS), expected October 2026, on top of a Higher Diploma in Agile Software Design and a B.Eng in Electrical & Electronics Engineering from Covenant University, Nigeria.
